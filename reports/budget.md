**Kerala 5-Day Travel Plan**

**Accommodation (15,000 INR)**

* Budget-friendly options: Guesthouses or hostels (avg. 3,000 INR per night)
* Alternative: Consider homestays or Airbnb for a local experience

**Food (10,000 INR)**

* Local cuisine: Try street food and traditional restaurants (avg. 500-1000 INR per meal)
* Mid-range options: Hotel restaurants or cafes (avg. 1,000-2,000 INR per meal)

**Transportation (8,000 INR)**

* Rent a scooter or bike for the duration of the trip (avg. 800-1,200 INR per day)
* Alternative: Use public transportation or autorickshaws (avg. 200-500 INR per ride)

**Activities (40,000 INR)**

* Explore Munnar and Thekkady: Tea plantations, waterfalls, and wildlife reserves
* Visit Alleppey backwaters for a relaxing houseboat stay (avg. 6,000-8,000 INR per night)
* Other activities: Trekking, visiting temples, and local markets (avg. 500-1,000 INR per person)

**Total Estimated Budget: 50,000 INR**

Note: Prices are estimates and may vary depending on individual preferences and exchange rates.
