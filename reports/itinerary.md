Here is a detailed 5-day itinerary for Kerala:

Day 1:
• Morning: Arrive in Kochi, explore the Fort area and St. Francis Church
• Afternoon: Take a houseboat cruise in Alleppey Backwaters
• Evening: Enjoy traditional Kerala cuisine and watch a Kathakali dance performance

Day 2:
• Morning: Drive to Munnar ( approx. 4 hours) and check-in at a tea plantation resort
• Afternoon: Explore the tea plantations, visit the Echo Point, and take in the scenic views
• Evening: Enjoy a traditional Kerala dinner and relax at the resort

Day 3:
• Morning: Trek to Anamudi Peak (2,695m)
• Afternoon: Visit the Munnar Tea Museum and learn about the history of tea in Kerala
• Evening: Return to Kochi for the night

Day 4:
• Morning: Drive to Thekkady (approx. 3 hours) and explore the Periyar National Park
• Afternoon: Take a boat tour in the park and spot wildlife
• Evening: Enjoy a relaxing evening at a resort near Thekkady

Day 5:
• Morning: Visit Athirappilly Falls and take a scenic hike to the waterfalls
• Afternoon: Drive back to Kochi (approx. 4 hours) for departure

This itinerary provides a mix of culture, nature, and adventure activities, but can be adjusted based on individual preferences and interests.
