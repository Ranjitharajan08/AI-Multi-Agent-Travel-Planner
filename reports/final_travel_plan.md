Here's a comprehensive travel plan for your 5-day Kerala adventure:

**Travel Plan: Kerala 5-Day Adventure**

**Day 1: Arrival in Kochi**

* Morning: Arrive at Kochi International Airport
* Check-in to hotel and freshen up
* Visit the Fort area and St. Francis Church (9:00 am - 12:00 pm)
* Houseboat cruise in Alleppey Backwaters (2:00 pm - 6:00 pm)
* Traditional Kerala dinner and Kathakali dance performance (7:30 pm - 10:30 pm)

**Day 2: Munnar**

* Morning: Drive to Munnar (approx. 4 hours)
* Check-in at tea plantation resort
* Explore the tea plantations, Echo Point, and scenic views (9:00 am - 12:00 pm)
* Traditional Kerala dinner and relaxation at resort (7:30 pm - 10:30 pm)

**Day 3: Anamudi Peak**

* Morning: Trek to Anamudi Peak (2,695m) (6:00 am - 8:00 am)
* Munnar Tea Museum visit and learn about tea history (9:00 am - 12:00 pm)
* Return to Kochi for the night

**Day 4: Thekkady**

* Morning: Drive to Thekkady (approx. 3 hours)
* Explore Periyar National Park by boat tour (9:00 am - 12:00 pm)
* Relaxation at resort near Thekkady
* Traditional Kerala dinner and cultural experience (7:30 pm - 10:30 pm)

**Day 5: Departure**

* Morning: Visit Athirappilly Falls and scenic hike to waterfalls (8:00 am - 11:00 am)
* Return to Kochi for departure

**Budget Breakdown:**

* Accommodation (15,000 INR): Budget-friendly options
* Food (10,000 INR): Local cuisine and mid-range options
* Transportation (8,000 INR): Rent a scooter or bike for the duration
* Activities (40,000 INR): Explore Munnar and Thekkady, visit Alleppey backwaters

**Total Estimated Budget: 50,000 INR**

**Travel Documents:**

* Passport with valid visa
* Flight tickets and hotel bookings confirmed
* Travel insurance documents
* Vaccination certificates

**Health and Safety:**

* Consult your doctor before traveling
* Bring necessary medications and personal care items
* Be aware of local customs and dress modestly when visiting temples or attending cultural events

**Local Guide:**

Your local guide will provide you with insider knowledge, cultural experiences, and safety tips to enhance your Kerala adventure.

**Language:**

English is widely spoken in Kerala; however, it's a good idea to learn basic Malayalam phrases to communicate with locals.

**Currency:**

Indian Rupees (INR) are the local currency. You can exchange currency at airports or hotels, or use ATMs to withdraw cash.
