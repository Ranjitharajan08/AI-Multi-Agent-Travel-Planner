**Kerala 5-Day Adventure Itinerary**

**Top Attractions:**
• Periyar National Park for wildlife spotting and boat tours
• Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary for trekking and birdwatching
• Athirappilly Falls for a scenic hike and waterfalls
• Munnar Tea Plantations for tea tastings and hill scenery

**Best Places to Visit:**
• Kochi (F fort, St. Francis Church)
• Alleppey Backwaters for houseboat cruises
• Thiruvananthapuram for Padmanabhaswamy Temple and beaches
• Idukki Dam for trekking and boat rides

**Must-see Experiences:**
• Kayaking or rafting in the backwaters of Alleppey
• Trekking to the top of Anamudi Peak (2,695m)
• Visiting a traditional Aayurvedic medicine center
• Watching a traditional Kathakali dance performance
